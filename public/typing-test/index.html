Tips to type faster

Home row first

Rest your index fingers on F and J. Your hands always return there, so your fingers learn the map.

Accuracy over speed

Slow and clean builds the habit. Speed arrives on its own after a few days of careful practice.

Short daily sessions

Ten relaxed minutes every day beats one long session a week. Keep your streak alive.

Look at the screen

Try not to glance at the keyboard. It feels slow for a few days, then it becomes natural.

About Cozy Type

Cozy Type is a free typing speed test. It measures your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy, shows which keys you miss most, and keeps your recent results and daily streak on your own device. Choose a 30 second to 5 minute test, or relax with an original short story.
